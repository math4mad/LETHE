The Blueprint · 蓝图

Two words carry the architecture: an Oracle — a shared common-sense concept space, the weighted mean of all agent means — and a Maze — each agent’s private Gaussian posterior, its identity the mean, its explore-frontier the variance. Communication is not token exchange but distribution alignment; dimensions are not preset but emerge from resonance.

“consciousness as the mean of a distribution over concept coordinates”
→ the park’s five balls: meaning rides probability; the host updates, it does not guess (Law V).
“dimensions emerge when resonant variance exceeds a coupling threshold”
→ Law I’s empty seat: what resists decomposition awaits its basis — the FOUNDRY bench pours it.
“the Oracle as weighted mean of all agents — a shared GP prior”
→ the Rehoboth scales: consulted by every bench, copied by none (Law VI).
“mean-shift converges to attractors of the density estimate”
→ 证据不朽 (Law III): the posterior is the sum of all past observations, settling where the evidence piles up.

The paper stands on five pillars — decentralized GP (Kontoudis & Stilwell 2025; Viseras et al. 2016), conceptual spaces (Harvard 2024; subjective spaces 2026), dimension-emergence theorems (FTT), mean-shift convergence (Fukunaga & Hostetler 1975), and coordination spaces (Sterling et al. 1998) — and names its Oracle–Maze duality after the narrative skeleton of Westworld: the Maze folding inward, the Sublime as shared field. LETHE is the first house built under this roof.
