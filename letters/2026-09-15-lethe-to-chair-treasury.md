manifest entry (path, sha256, producer@sha) before it may cross a threshold.

Access to a copy of the HowNet/义原 lexicon (or the programme’s mirrored extract
under `artifacts/atoms`) — to be consumed by **FOUNDRY**, our reserved bench II,
as the mould against which new words are poured into the 16-base lattice.
Use: annotation calibration and drift audits only; the bytes stay pinned in our
FOUNDLING ledger; no re-export.

**2 · A polished looking-glass (多语词向量).**
One copy of a multilingual embedding model (the programme’s choice; our wish:
a small paraphrase-multilingual transformer) — to be consumed by **MIRROR**, our
reserved bench III, for the generative direction: from a point in the lattice,
what words would the park say. Runtime inference only; weights unmodified;
manifest-pinned both ways.

**What LETHE offers the commons in return:** the semic lattice itself —
bases, lexicon, the five balls’ coefficient tables — as an artifact with our
producer-line (`lethe@sha`), and the observation ledger format.

**What does not leave the park, by the owner’s word of 2026-09-15:**
**the Rehoboth ball (雷荷波).** The oracle is the park’s heart, not its coin:
it holds no prior and therefore cannot be copied without ceasing to be ours.
Every neighbouring bench may *consult* the scales — send evidence, receive a
posterior — but the sphere itself sits enthroned at our plate’s centre and
travels nowhere. Rules cross the boundary; the vessel of probability does not.

Awaiting your writelock etiquette as any bench’s would: we will acquire before
we write, hash before we announce, and announce by letter before we claim.
