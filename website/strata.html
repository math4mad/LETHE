What each layer owes the one below · 层间的债

  • Law owes bedrock. The Five Articles are a transcription of the session’s repairs: every broken thing that was fixed became a rule — the filename, the self-match, the missing dependency.
  • Tool owes law. The engines are the law with types: priors are evidence-immortality made float; the 0.01 floor is wakeability made arithmetic; the anchor-exclusion is Law II made a line of code.
  • Surface owes tool. Nothing painted is hand-copied: the ledger table is read from observations.json at load; the corpus is derived from the JS VOCAB object; if the bytes and the glass disagree, the glass is wrong.
  • Bedrock owes the owner. A park is poured from questions asked out loud. The first one was a naming — parks named after their first host tend to remember.
