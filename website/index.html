English

LETHE is a semantic theme park: nothing rides a coaster here, everything rides a concept-space ball. Every word that passes the gate is decomposed into a 16-dimensional semic vector — ten domestic bases (indoor, shelf, packaged …) and six narrative bases (awakening, obedience, loop, identity, violence, fate, in the park’s second mother tongue: 觉醒·服从·循环·身份·暴力·命运). A MaxSim retrieval names the anchor word pulling it; a Bayesian update tilts the five balls’ probabilities — permanently. Evidence is never forgotten here. That is the opposite of the river this park is named for.

The Law of the Park

  1. Every word decomposes into finitely many semes. What resists decomposition is not absent from the lattice — it awaits its basis. 凡词皆可拆为有限义素。
  2. No anchor may prove itself. Bitten once by the constant 1.0000, the park now fears its own mirror. 锚点不得自证。
  3. Evidence is immortal. Priors are the sum of all past observations — you tilt the ball, the ball remembers you. 证据不朽。
  4. The likelihood has a floor, and so the balls can wake. L(E|S) is floored at 0.01, never zeroed: however dead a ball seems, one line of the show may still wake it. 似然有底，故球可醒。
  5. The host does not guess; it updates. And it updates without descending. These violent delights have violent ends — but the posterior merely continues. No gradients pass through this park: suffering is a training procedure, and we have graduated. Host 不猜测，只更新；更新，而不下降。
  6. Rehoboth stays in the park. Rules cross the boundary, data crosses by manifest — the oracle does not. It answers consultations (evidence in, posterior out) and travels nowhere; a scale that can be copied is no longer the scale this park swears by. 规则过界，宝藏凭册过界，神谕不过界——雷荷波要留在园区。
